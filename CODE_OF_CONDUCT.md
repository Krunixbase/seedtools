# Code of Conduct — SeedTools Suite

SeedTools Suite is an open‑source project built as a **public good** for individuals, NGOs, and high‑risk communities.  
To maintain a safe, inclusive, and productive environment, all contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

This document is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant.

---

## 1. Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the SeedTools community a **harassment‑free experience** for everyone, regardless of:

- age  
- disability  
- ethnicity  
- gender identity or expression  
- level of experience  
- nationality  
- personal appearance  
- race  
- religion  
- sexual identity or orientation  

We commit to fostering an environment where **everyone feels welcome**.

---

## 2. Our Standards

### Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness  
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints  
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback  
- Taking responsibility for mistakes  
- Focusing on what is best for the community  

### Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments  
- Public or private intimidation  
- Publishing others’ private information  
- Disruptive or aggressive behavior  
- Any conduct that violates privacy or safety  

---

## 3. Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- clarifying standards of acceptable behavior  
- reviewing and moderating contributions  
- taking appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior  
- removing or rejecting contributions that violate this Code  

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to **protect the project and its community**.

---

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- the project repository  
- GitHub issues and pull requests  
- community spaces (forums, chats, discussions)  
- private communication related to the project  

---

## 5. Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

**krunixbase@gmail.com**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of reporters.

---

## 6. Enforcement Guidelines

### 1. Correction  
**Consequence:** A private, written warning.

### 2. Warning  
**Consequence:** A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.

### 3. Temporary Ban  
**Consequence:** Temporary removal from community spaces.

### 4. Permanent Ban  
**Consequence:** Permanent removal from the project community.

---
