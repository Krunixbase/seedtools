# **Entropy Inspector — Roadmap**

The Entropy Inspector is the **low‑level diagnostic engine** of the SeedTools Suite.  
Its mission is to provide **deterministic, offline‑first entropy validation**, checksum reconstruction, drift detection, and corruption analysis — essential for safe recovery and forensic workflows.

---

# **1. High‑Level Vision**

The Entropy Inspector becomes:

- the **global standard** for Bitcoin entropy diagnostics  
- the **reference implementation** for checksum and drift analysis  
- the **trusted NGO tool** for verifying compromised backups  
- the **core engine** powering Recovery and Forensics  
- the **educational tool** for understanding entropy → mnemonic mapping  

---

# **2. 3‑Month Roadmap — Foundation Phase**

- **Entropy Validation Engine v1**  
  Bit‑length checks, entropy → mnemonic mapping, checksum extraction.

- **Checksum Analyzer MVP**  
  Validate checksum bits, detect mismatches, highlight incorrect segments.

- **Entropy Reconstruction Preview**  
  Show raw entropy, checksum bits, and mnemonic mapping.

- **Mnemonic Structure Inspector**  
  Wordlist conformity, invalid‑word detection, word count validation.

- **Entropy Drift Detection MVP**  
  Detect bit‑level drift and entropy inconsistencies.

- **Documentation v1**  
  Architecture, examples, entropy theory, checksum logic.

---

# **3. 6‑Month Roadmap — Expansion Phase**

- **Advanced Drift Detection**  
  Detect subtle drift patterns, multi‑segment drift, entropy fingerprinting.

- **Partial Entropy Reconstruction**  
  Assist users with partially corrupted mnemonics or missing words.

- **Taproot‑Specific Entropy Checks**  
  BIP86‑related entropy validation and checksum mapping.

- **Entropy‑to‑Mnemonic Visualizer**  
  Visual mapping of entropy bits → mnemonic words.

- **UX Enhancements**  
  Drift heatmaps, checksum indicators, educational tooltips.

- **Export Module v1**  
  Entropy reports, drift maps, checksum summaries.

---

# **4. 12‑Month Roadmap — PRO Phase**

- **Entropy Inspector PRO**  
  Full drift maps, corruption scoring, anomaly classification.

- **Compromised Backup Workflow**  
  Safe workflows for tampered, partially destroyed, or suspicious backups.

- **NGO‑Friendly Reports**  
  Printable, minimal‑metadata, field‑safe reports.

- **Interoperability Layer**  
  Shared entropy models across Recovery, Forensics, and CLI.

- **Integration with Forensics Tool**  
  Automatic entropy diagnostics during forensic scans.

- **Reproducible Builds**  
  Deterministic binaries + verification instructions.

---

# **5. Strategic Themes**

- **Determinism** — reproducible entropy analysis, transparent bit‑level logic  
- **Security** — offline‑first, hardened mode, zero‑trace execution  
- **Interoperability** — shared entropy models across all SeedTools apps  
- **Accessibility** — visual entropy mapping, guided explanations  
- **Research‑Grade Accuracy** — drift scoring, anomaly signatures  

---

# **6. Long‑Term Vision**

The Entropy Inspector becomes:

- the **trusted global standard** for entropy diagnostics  
- the **primary tool** for NGOs verifying compromised backups  
- the **reference implementation** for entropy → mnemonic mapping  
- the **core forensic engine** used by researchers and developers  
- fully **offline**, **auditable**, **reproducible**, and **secure**
