# **Forensics Tool — Roadmap**

The Forensics Tool is the **diagnostic and analytical engine** of the SeedTools Suite.  
Its mission is to provide **deterministic, offline‑first forensic analysis** of mnemonics, entropy, derivation paths, and wallet structures — safe for NGOs, high‑risk users, and security researchers.

---

# **1. High‑Level Vision**

The Forensics Tool becomes:

- the **global standard** for Bitcoin seed forensics  
- the **reference implementation** for entropy drift and corruption analysis  
- the **trusted NGO tool** for field diagnostics  
- the **auditable, deterministic engine** used by researchers and developers  
- the **foundation** for safe recovery workflows  

---

# **2. 3‑Month Roadmap — Foundation Phase**

- **Forensics Engine v1**  
  Core logic for entropy reconstruction, checksum validation, and mnemonic structure analysis.

- **Entropy Drift Detection MVP**  
  Detect bit‑level inconsistencies and entropy anomalies.

- **Corruption Mapping MVP**  
  Identify invalid words, checksum mismatches, and structural errors.

- **Mnemonic Integrity Engine v1**  
  Wordlist conformity, checksum verification, entropy length checks.

- **Forensic Report v1**  
  Human‑readable, offline‑safe forensic summaries.

- **Documentation v1**  
  Architecture, workflows, examples, forensic methodology.

---

# **3. 6‑Month Roadmap — Expansion Phase**

- **Advanced Drift Detection**  
  Detect subtle entropy inconsistencies and pattern‑based anomalies.

- **Partial Mnemonic Reconstruction**  
  Assist users with missing, corrupted, or uncertain words.

- **Taproot Forensics (BIP86)**  
  Validate P2TR derivation paths, entropy, and key structure.

- **Wallet Metadata Analyzer**  
  Analyze Sparrow, Specter, Electrum, and BTCPay metadata for inconsistencies.

- **Deep Path Forensics Engine**  
  Multi‑account anomaly detection, extended index ranges.

- **UX Enhancements**  
  Visual drift maps, corruption heatmaps, guided explanations.

---

# **4. 12‑Month Roadmap — PRO Phase**

- **Forensics Mode PRO**  
  Full corruption analysis, entropy drift maps, anomaly scoring, mixed‑wallet detection.

- **Compromised Device Workflow**  
  Safe workflows for confiscated, tampered, or malware‑infected devices.

- **NGO‑Friendly Forensic Reports**  
  Printable, field‑safe, minimal‑metadata reports.

- **Interoperability Layer**  
  Shared forensic models across Desktop, CLI, and Launcher.

- **Integration with Recovery Tool**  
  Automatic anomaly detection during recovery workflows.

- **Reproducible Builds**  
  Deterministic binaries + verification instructions.

---

# **5. Strategic Themes**

- **Determinism** — reproducible forensic results, transparent algorithms

- **Interoperability** — shared forensic models across all apps  
- **Accessibility** — guided explanations, NGO presets, visual diagnostics  
- **Research‑Grade Accuracy** — entropy drift scoring, anomaly signatures  

---

# **6. Long‑Term Vision**

The Forensics Tool becomes:

- the **trusted global standard** for Bitcoin seed forensics  
- the **primary diagnostic tool** for NGOs and support teams  
- the **reference implementation** for entropy and mnemonic analysis  
- the **auditable, deterministic engine** used by researchers
