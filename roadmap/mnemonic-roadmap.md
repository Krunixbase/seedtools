# **Mnemonic Tools — Roadmap**

The Mnemonic Tools module is the **core BIP39 analysis and transformation engine** of the SeedTools Suite.  
Its mission is to provide **deterministic, offline‑first mnemonic validation**, entropy conversion, checksum inspection, and structural analysis — essential for safe recovery, forensics, and education.

---

# **1. High‑Level Vision**

Mnemonic Tools becomes:

- the **global reference implementation** for BIP39 mnemonic logic  
- the **safest offline mnemonic analysis tool**  
- the **educational engine** for understanding entropy, checksum, and wordlists  
- the **foundation** for Recovery, Forensics, and Entropy Inspector  
- the **trusted NGO tool** for verifying compromised backups  

---

# **2. 3‑Month Roadmap — Foundation Phase**





- **Mnemonic Validation Engine v1**  
  Wordlist conformity, checksum verification, word count validation.

- **Entropy Conversion MVP**  
  Mnemonic → entropy → checksum bits.

- **Checksum Inspector MVP**  
  Detect mismatches, highlight incorrect segments.

- **Wordlist Tools v1**  
  Word index lookup, invalid‑word detection, language‑agnostic structure.

- **Mnemonic Metadata Engine**  
  Word count, entropy size, checksum size, structural integrity.

- **Documentation v1**  
  BIP39 theory, entropy mapping, checksum logic.

---

# **3. 6‑Month Roadmap — Expansion Phase**





- **Advanced Checksum Analysis**  
  Bit‑level mismatch detection, checksum drift scoring.

- **Partial Mnemonic Reconstruction**  
  Assist users with missing or uncertain words.

- **Entropy‑to‑Mnemonic Visualizer**  
  Visual mapping of entropy bits → mnemonic words.

- **Taproot‑Aware Mnemonic Checks**  
  BIP86‑related entropy and checksum validation.

- **UX Enhancements**  
  Visual indicators, warnings, educational tooltips.

- **Export Module v1**  
  Mnemonic reports, entropy summaries, checksum maps.

---

# **4. 12‑Month Roadmap — PRO Phase**





- **Mnemonic Tools PRO**  
  Full corruption scoring, drift detection, anomaly classification.

- **Compromised Backup Workflow**  
  Safe workflows for tampered, partially destroyed, or suspicious mnemonics.

- **NGO‑Friendly Reports**  
  Printable, minimal‑metadata, field‑safe summaries.

- **Interoperability Layer**  
  Shared mnemonic models across Recovery, Forensics, Entropy Inspector.

- **Integration with Recovery Tool**  
  Automatic mnemonic diagnostics during recovery workflows.

- **Reproducible Builds**  
  Deterministic binaries + verification instructions.
