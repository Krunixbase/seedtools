# **Entropy Inspector — Roadmap**

## **Overview**

This roadmap defines the development plan for the Entropy Inspector over the next 12 months.
The focus is on entropy diagnostics, checksum analysis, drift detection, mnemonic structure validation, educational tooling, and integration with the Forensics Tool.

---

# **1. 3‑Month Roadmap — Foundation Phase**

- **Entropy Validation Engine v1** — entropy reconstruction, bit‑length checks, BIP39 compliance
- **Checksum Analyzer MVP** — checksum validation, mismatch detection, incorrect‑bit mapping
- **Mnemonic Structure Inspector** — wordlist conformity, invalid‑word detection, word count validation
- **Entropy Reconstruction Preview** — raw entropy, checksum bits, mnemonic mapping
- **Documentation v1** — workflows, examples, architecture

---

# **2. 6‑Month Roadmap — Expansion Phase**

- **Advanced Drift Detection** — detect subtle entropy drift, highlight drifted segments
- **Partial Entropy Reconstruction** — assist users with partially corrupted mnemonics
- **Taproot‑Specific Entropy Checks** — BIP86‑related entropy validation
- **Entropy‑to‑Mnemonic Visualizer** — visual mapping of entropy bits to mnemonic words
- **UX Enhancements** — clearer warnings, drift indicators, educational explanations
- **Export Module** — export entropy reports for NGOs/support teams

---

# **3. 12‑Month Roadmap — PRO Phase**

- **Entropy Inspector PRO** — full drift maps, corruption scoring, anomaly classification
- **Compromised Backup Workflow** — safe workflows for tampered or partially destroyed backups
- **NGO‑Friendly Reports** — printable, minimal‑metadata, field‑safe reports
- **Interoperability Layer** — compatibility with wallet metadata formats
- **Integration with Forensics Tool** — automatic entropy diagnostics during forensic scans
- **Reproducible Builds** — deterministic builds + verification instructions

---

# **Long‑Term Vision**

Entropy Inspector becomes the **standard offline entropy diagnostic tool** for Bitcoin:

- trusted by NGOs and support teams
- used by wallet developers to validate entropy handling
- safe for high‑risk users
- fully auditable and reproducible
- maintained as a long‑term public good
