# **1. Project Summary**

SeedTools 2.0 is an **offline, open‑source toolkit** that enables safe recovery of Bitcoin HD wallets, analysis of corrupted mnemonics, and forensic verification of addresses and derivation paths.
It is designed specifically for **activists, journalists, and high‑risk users** operating in authoritarian environments where digital security is critical.

SeedTools provides deterministic, transparent, air‑gapped workflows that allow users to recover funds **without exposing private data** or relying on third parties.

---

# **2. Problem Statement**

Millions of Bitcoin users — especially those in repressive regimes — lose access to their funds due to:

- corrupted or incomplete mnemonics
- wrong derivation paths
- missing passphrases
- compromised or confiscated devices
- lack of safe offline tools
- reliance on custodial or online recovery services

For activists and journalists, these failures can be catastrophic:

- loss of operational funds
- exposure of sensitive information
- inability to support their communities
- increased vulnerability to state surveillance

There is **no unified, offline‑first, open‑source toolkit** that provides deterministic wallet recovery and forensic analysis in a safe, transparent way.

SeedTools fills this gap.

---

# **3. How SeedTools Helps Human Rights**

SeedTools directly supports human rights by enabling:

### **Secure self‑custody for activists**
Offline recovery tools reduce reliance on custodial services that may be monitored or coerced.

### **Safe recovery after device confiscation**
Forensics Mode helps users determine whether a device or mnemonic has been tampered with.

### **Protection from surveillance**
SeedTools runs fully offline — no telemetry, no API calls, no network stack.

### **Operational continuity**
NGOs and journalists can recover funds even when backups are damaged or incomplete.

### **Education & empowerment**
SeedTools provides transparent, auditable workflows that teach users how Bitcoin wallets actually work.
