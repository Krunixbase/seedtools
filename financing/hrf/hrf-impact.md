# **SeedTools 2.0 — Human Rights Impact Statement**

## **1. Overview**

SeedTools 2.0 is designed to support **activists, journalists, dissidents, and NGOs** operating in hostile environments where digital security is essential.
The toolkit provides **offline, transparent, deterministic Bitcoin wallet recovery**, enabling users to regain access to funds without exposing sensitive information or relying on third parties.

SeedTools strengthens **financial autonomy**, **privacy**, and **operational security** — all critical pillars of human rights.

---

# **2. How SeedTools Protects High‑Risk Users**

## **2.1 Offline‑First Architecture**
SeedTools runs fully offline:

- no telemetry
- no analytics
- no API calls
- no network stack

This protects users from:

- surveillance
- metadata leaks
- ISP‑level monitoring
- state‑level interception

Offline workflows are essential for activists in authoritarian regimes.

---

## **2.2 Safe Recovery After Device Confiscation**
When a phone or laptop is seized:

- mnemonics may be copied
- wallets may be tampered with
- backups may be corrupted
- passphrases may be extracted under coercion

SeedTools provides:

- forensic analysis of mnemonic integrity
- entropy drift detection
- derivation path verification
- address exposure analysis

This helps users determine whether their funds are still safe.

---

## **2.3 Protection From Coercion & Surveillance**
SeedTools enables:

- air‑gapped recovery
- no‑disk‑write Safe Mode
- secure memory handling
- deterministic workflows that leave no traces

This reduces the risk of:

- forced wallet recovery
- forensic extraction
- digital evidence trails

---

## **2.4 Empowering NGOs and Journalists**
NGOs often assist users who:

- lost access to funds
- have corrupted backups
- operate under threat
- cannot trust custodial services

SeedTools gives NGOs:

- transparent recovery tools
- deterministic logic they can audit
- offline workflows safe for field operations
- reproducible builds they can verify
