# **SeedTools 2.0 — One‑Pager (HRF Edition)**

## **What is SeedTools?**
SeedTools 2.0 is an **offline, open‑source toolkit** that helps activists, journalists, and high‑risk users safely recover Bitcoin wallets, analyze corrupted mnemonics, and verify addresses without exposing sensitive data.

It is designed for people operating in **authoritarian environments**, where digital security and privacy are essential for survival.

---

## **Why It Matters for Human Rights**
Bitcoin is a lifeline for many activists and journalists.
But losing access to funds due to corrupted backups or compromised devices can be devastating.

SeedTools protects human rights by enabling:

- **safe recovery after device confiscation**
- **offline workflows resistant to surveillance**
- **secure self‑custody without third parties**
- **transparent, auditable recovery logic**
- **operational continuity for NGOs and dissidents**

SeedTools empowers people who rely on Bitcoin for freedom, safety, and independence.

---

## **Key Features**

- deterministic HD wallet recovery
- deep derivation path scanning (0 → X)
- BIP44/49/84/86 + Taproot
- corrupted mnemonic analysis
- entropy validation
- Safe Mode for activists
- CLI for Tails/Qubes
- fully offline (no telemetry, no API calls)
