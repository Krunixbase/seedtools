# **SeedTools Suite — One‑Pager**

## **What is SeedTools Suite?**

SeedTools Suite is a **deterministic, offline‑first Bitcoin recovery and forensics toolkit** designed for individuals, NGOs, and support teams operating in **high‑risk environments**.  
It provides **transparent algorithms**, **reproducible outputs**, and **hardened security workflows** for users who cannot afford mistakes.

---

## **The Problem**

Millions of Bitcoin users face irreversible loss due to:

- corrupted or incomplete mnemonics  
- entropy drift and checksum mismatches  
- unknown or mixed derivation paths  
- confiscated or tampered devices  
- unsafe online recovery tools  
- lack of secure workflows for NGOs  

Bitcoin is unforgiving — one wrong step can mean permanent loss.

---

## **The Solution**

SeedTools Suite delivers a **modular, deterministic, offline‑safe ecosystem**:

- **Recovery Tool** — deterministic wallet reconstruction  
- **Forensics Tool** — corruption mapping, drift detection  
- **Entropy Inspector** — bit‑level entropy diagnostics  
- **Mnemonic Tools** — validation, reconstruction, entropy conversion  
- **Path Explorer** — BIP32/44/49/84/86 analysis  
- **Address Scanner** — batch verification & path inference  
- **Desktop App** — guided workflows for non‑technical users  
- **CLI** — automation & reproducible pipelines  
- **Suite Launcher** — unified entry point  

All modules operate **fully offline**, with **zero telemetry** and **reproducible builds**.
