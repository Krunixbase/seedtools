 **Executive Summary — SeedTools Suite**

SeedTools Suite is a **deterministic, offline‑first, security‑focused toolkit** for Bitcoin seed recovery, forensics, entropy diagnostics, and wallet structure analysis.  
It is designed for individuals, NGOs, support teams, and researchers operating in **high‑risk or high‑integrity environments**, where safety, transparency, and reproducibility are essential.

---

## **1. Mission**

To provide the **safest, most transparent, and most deterministic** open‑source toolkit for Bitcoin recovery and forensics — built as a **public good** for users who cannot afford mistakes.

---

## **2. The Problem**

Millions of Bitcoin users face irreversible loss due to:

- corrupted mnemonics  
- missing or uncertain words  
- entropy drift  
- checksum mismatches  
- unknown derivation paths  
- mixed‑wallet structures  
- compromised backups  
- confiscated or tampered devices  

Existing tools are fragmented, online‑dependent, opaque, or unsafe for high‑risk users.

---

## **3. The Solution — SeedTools Suite**

SeedTools Suite provides a **unified, deterministic, offline‑safe ecosystem** of tools:

- **Recovery Tool** — deterministic wallet reconstruction  
- **Forensics Tool** — corruption analysis, drift detection  
- **Entropy Inspector** — bit‑level entropy diagnostics  
- **Mnemonic Tools** — checksum validation, partial reconstruction  
- **Path Explorer** — BIP32/44/49/84/86 path analysis  
- **Address Scanner** — batch verification & path inference  
- **Desktop App** — guided workflows  
- **CLI** — automation & reproducible pipelines  
- **Suite Launcher** — unified entry point  

All modules share a **zero‑trust, offline‑first, reproducible** architecture.
