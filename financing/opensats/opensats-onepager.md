# **SeedTools 2.0 — One‑Pager**

## **What is SeedTools?**  
SeedTools 2.0 is an **offline, open‑source toolkit** for recovering Bitcoin HD wallets, analyzing corrupted backups, scanning derivation paths, and verifying addresses.  
It is designed for high‑risk users, activists, journalists, and NGOs who need **secure, private, air‑gapped wallet recovery tools**.

---

## **Why It Matters**  
Millions of users lose access to Bitcoin due to:

- wrong derivation paths  
- corrupted mnemonics  
- missing passphrases  
- incompatible wallet formats  
- compromised devices  
- lack of offline tools  

SeedTools solves this by providing **deterministic, transparent, offline‑first recovery workflows**.

---

## **Key Features**

- deterministic HD wallet recovery  
- deep derivation path scanning (0 → 100 500)  
- BIP44/49/84/86 + Taproot  
- mnemonic integrity & repair  
- address verification  
- forensics mode for compromised devices  
- GUI + CLI + Core modules  
- fully offline (no telemetry, no API calls)

---

## **Who Benefits**

- activists & journalists in hostile regions  
- NGOs assisting users with compromised devices  
- self‑custody beginners  
- advanced users recovering old wallets  
- Bitcoin educators & support teams  

---

## **Impact on Bitcoin**

SeedTools strengthens Bitcoin self‑custody by:

- reducing permanent loss of funds  
- enabling safe recovery without trusted third parties  
- improving wallet interoperability  
- supporting high‑risk users globally  
- providing transparent, auditable recovery logic
