## 1. Project Summary

SeedTools Suite is an offline-first, open-source Bitcoin recovery, forensics, entropy analysis, and security platform.

The project helps users recover Bitcoin wallets, analyze corrupted backups, verify derivation paths, validate addresses, and investigate recovery-related incidents without exposing sensitive information to third parties.

SeedTools Suite is designed around transparency, determinism, privacy, and reproducibility. All core workflows operate offline and are auditable by users and contributors.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Many modern software systems require users to trust opaque services, online infrastructure, and closed ecosystems.

For Bitcoin users, this creates additional challenges:

- wallet recovery often depends on fragmented tooling
- many workflows require online access
- auditability is limited
- deterministic verification is difficult
- high-risk users require stronger privacy guarantees

When users lose access to wallets, backups become corrupted, or derivation paths are unknown, existing solutions are often complex, incomplete, or dependent on third-party services.

Users should be able to verify, recover, and analyze their own data without surrendering privacy or control.

SeedTools Suite provides:
