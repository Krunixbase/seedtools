## **SeedTools Suite — One-Pager (FUTO Edition)**

### **What is SeedTools?**

SeedTools Suite is an offline-first, open-source Bitcoin recovery, forensics,
entropy analysis, and security platform.

It helps users recover Bitcoin wallets, analyze corrupted backups,
verify derivation paths, validate addresses, and perform deterministic
security verification workflows without relying on third-party services.

SeedTools is designed around transparency, auditability, privacy,
and user ownership.

---

### **Why It Matters**

Modern users increasingly depend on software they cannot inspect,
verify, or control.

Critical security workflows often require:

- online services
- centralized platforms
- opaque algorithms
- external trust assumptions

SeedTools takes a different approach.

Every workflow is designed to be:

- deterministic
- transparent
- reproducible
- offline-first
- user-controlled

The goal is to give users ownership of recovery and verification
processes without depending on third parties.

### **Key Features**
