# **Impact Statement — SeedTools Suite**

SeedTools Suite delivers **high‑integrity, offline‑first, deterministic Bitcoin recovery and forensics** for individuals, NGOs, and support teams operating in high‑risk environments.  
Its impact is measured not only in technical correctness, but in **human safety, financial resilience, and long‑term preservation of digital autonomy**.

---

## **1. Protecting High‑Risk Users**

Millions of people rely on Bitcoin as a lifeline in environments where:

- bank accounts are frozen  
- surveillance is pervasive  
- devices are confiscated  
- backups are tampered with  
- internet access is restricted  

SeedTools provides:

- **offline‑only workflows** safe from surveillance  
- **zero‑trace execution** that leaves no digital footprint  
- **hardened security modes** for confiscation‑risk scenarios  
- **guided recovery flows** usable by non‑technical individuals  

This directly supports:

- activists  
- journalists  
- dissidents  
- refugees  
- human‑rights defenders  
- people escaping authoritarian regimes  

👉 **NGO Security Presets**

---

## **2. Reducing Irreversible Bitcoin Loss**

Bitcoin is unforgiving.  
A single corrupted mnemonic or wrong derivation path can mean **permanent loss**.

SeedTools mitigates this by providing:

- deterministic recovery  
- entropy drift detection  
- mnemonic integrity checks  
- path inference  
- address verification  
- forensic diagnostics  

This dramatically reduces the number of users who lose access to their funds due to:

- corrupted backups  
- missing words  
- drifted entropy  
- mixed‑wallet structures  
- incorrect metadata
