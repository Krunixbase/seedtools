# **Product Overview — SeedTools Suite**

## **1. What is SeedTools Suite**

SeedTools Suite is a **deterministic, offline‑first, security‑focused toolkit** for Bitcoin seed recovery, forensics, diagnostics, and education.  
It is designed for:

- individuals recovering their own wallets  
- NGOs operating in high‑risk environments  
- support teams assisting users with compromised backups  
- researchers and developers analyzing BIP standards  
- auditors verifying deterministic wallet behavior  

SeedTools Suite provides a **modular, transparent, reproducible** environment that prioritizes user safety, correctness, and auditability.

---

## **2. Why SeedTools Exists**

Bitcoin users lose access to funds due to:

- corrupted mnemonics  
- missing words  
- drifted entropy  
- unknown derivation paths  
- mixed‑wallet structures  
- incorrect wallet metadata  
- compromised or tampered backups  

Existing tools are fragmented, online‑dependent, or unsafe for high‑risk users.

SeedTools Suite solves this by offering:

- **one unified, offline, deterministic toolkit**  
- **transparent algorithms** with reproducible outputs  
- **modular engines** for recovery, forensics, entropy, mnemonic, path, and address analysis  
- **NGO‑ready workflows** for field operations  
- **zero‑trust architecture** with hardened security modes

## **6. Key Problems Solved**

SeedTools Suite addresses the most common and most dangerous wallet issues:
