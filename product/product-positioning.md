# **Product Positioning — SeedTools Suite**

SeedTools Suite positions itself as the **global standard for deterministic, offline‑first Bitcoin recovery and forensics**, designed for individuals, NGOs, support teams, and researchers operating in high‑risk or high‑integrity environments.

---

## **1. Market Positioning Summary**

SeedTools occupies a **unique, unclaimed space** in the Bitcoin tooling ecosystem:

- **Not a wallet**  
- **Not a seed brute‑forcer**  
- **Not a cloud service**  
- **Not a blockchain explorer**

Instead, SeedTools is positioned as:

> **A deterministic, offline, security‑first recovery and forensics suite for Bitcoin seeds, entropy, paths, and addresses.**

This makes it the **first toolkit** built specifically for:
- safe recovery  
- forensic diagnostics  
- NGO field operations  
- deterministic auditing  
- education and research
