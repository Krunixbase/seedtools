# **SeedTools Suite — Product Vision**

SeedTools Suite is a **deterministic, offline‑first recovery and forensics platform** designed for individuals, NGOs, and high‑risk users who need to safely recover, inspect, and validate Bitcoin wallets — even in hostile environments.

Its mission is simple:

> **Make Bitcoin recovery safe, deterministic, transparent, and accessible — for everyone.**

SeedTools Suite is built on three pillars:

- **Security** — no telemetry, no external calls, hardened memory, offline‑first  
- **Determinism** — reproducible results, transparent logic, verifiable outputs  
- **Clarity** — clean UX, guided workflows, educational value  

---

# **1. Why SeedTools Exists**

Bitcoin self‑custody is powerful — but fragile.  
People lose access to their funds because of:

- corrupted mnemonics  
- forgotten passphrases  
- wrong derivation paths  
- wallet migrations  
- inconsistent wallet standards  
- lack of safe recovery tools  
- unsafe online “recovery services”  

SeedTools exists to solve this.

It provides **trusted, auditable, deterministic tools** that anyone can use **offline**, without risk of data leakage or manipulation.

---

# **2. What SeedTools Aims to Become**

SeedTools Suite aims to be the **global standard** for:

- deterministic wallet recovery  
- mnemonic and entropy forensics  
- derivation path exploration  
- address scanning and verification  
- safe onboarding for NGOs and high‑risk users  
- reproducible, verifiable Bitcoin tooling  

SeedTools should be the tool people reach for when:

- a developer needs deterministic test vectors  
- a security researcher needs transparent logic
