#📘 SeedTools Brandbook**

---

## **1. Introduction**
SeedTools Suite is an **offline Bitcoin recovery toolkit**, designed for professionals, auditors, service technicians, and security-conscious users. SeedTools branding must be:

- **technical** but accessible
- **minimalist** but distinctive
- **neutral** but with a strong DNA
- **secure** because it concerns critical data

SeedTools is a tool that inspires **trust**, **peace of mind**, and **control**.

--

## **2. SeedTools Logo**

### **2.1 Design**
The logo consists of:

- Symbol: **hex‑node network** (core + branches)
- Logotype: "SeedTools"
- Optional tagline: "Offline Bitcoin Recovery Toolkit"

The symbol represents:

- Data structure
- Deterministicity
- Security
- Modularity

---

### **2.2 Logo Variants**
- **Mono (currentColor)** — Main variant
- **Dark** — For light backgrounds
- **Light** — For dark backgrounds
- **Mark** — Symbol only, no text
- **Horizontal** — Symbol + text on one line
- **Stacked** — Symbol above text

---

### **2.3 Safeguard (safe) area)**
Minimum margin around the logo = height of the letter **S** in the logotype.

--

### **2.4 Minimum size**
- Full logo: **120 px** width
- Symbol (mark): **32 px**

---

### **2.5 Prohibited modifications**
- changing proportions
- changing line thickness
- adding effects (shadows, gradients)
- rotating the symbol
- changing colors outside the brand palette

---

## **4. Typography**

### **4.1 Primary Fonts**
- **Inter** — UI, documentation, web
- **JetBrains Mono** — code, CLI, technical snippets

### **4.2 Hierarchy**
- H1 — Inter Bold
- H2 — Inter Semibold
- H3 — Inter Medium
- Body — Inter Regular
- Code — JetBrains Mono Regular

## **6. Logo Usage Rules**

### **6.1 On a Light Background**
Use the **dark** or **mono** variant.

### **6.2 On a Dark Background**
Use the **light** variant.

### **6.3 In Photos**
Only the **mono** version with white or black.

## **7. Social Preview (GitHub / Twitter / Docs)**

### **Format**

- logo + tagline
- minimalism, plenty of space

---

## **8. Tone of Voice**

SeedTools communicates:

- **technical** but understandable
- **unemotional** but not cold
- **no marketing bullshit**
- **with an emphasis on security and precision**

Tone of Voice Example:

> "SeedTools works completely offline.
> User data never leaves the device."

--

## **9. Correct/Incorrect Examples**

### **Correct**
- monochrome logo on a white background
- symbol as a favicon
- currentColor in the UI

### **Incorrect**
- gradients
- aspect ratio change
- color outside the palette
- logo outline
